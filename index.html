Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Safari deployment, hardware compatibility, and USALI certification.

USALI stands for the Uniform System of Accounts for the Lodging Industry. Unlike general accounting packages (like QuickBooks or generic ERPs), Safari’s Chart of Accounts has been designed and certified in consultation with Chartered Accountants (CAs). Operating statements for Rooms (Schedule 1), F&B (Schedule 2), and A&G (Schedule 4) populate automatically, calculating Gross Operating Profit (GOP) in accordance with international hospitality hotel standards.
Safari utilizes a local edge caching engine. If your internet connection drops, POS terminals continue printing kitchen order tickets (KOTs), generating guest bills, and storing transactions locally on the device. Once connectivity is restored, all data automatically syncs back to the cloud database with zero duplicate transactions or lost orders.
Our deployment team uses automated database migration scripts that extract your existing guest history, room categories, rate tables, and F&B recipe menus from your legacy system. While your current system remains live, we configure the hardware and conduct staff training in an isolated sandbox. On Day 8, we do a midnight cutover during low-occupancy night audit, ensuring seamless continuity.
