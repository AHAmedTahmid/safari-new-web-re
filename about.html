The Genesis Story

Born Out of Frustration with Disconnected Legacy Software

For decades, hospitality operators, super shops, and event venues were forced to stitch together 4 to 6 disparate software packages: one vendor for point-of-sale checkout, another for hotel room inventory, spreadsheets for banquet catering, and a generic accounting tool that had never heard of USALI.

The result? Constant checkout freezes during internet drops, double-booked banquet halls, staff wasting 4+ hours every night on manual reconciliation, and financial reports that arrived weeks after the month closed.

We built Safari to prove that enterprise hospitality software can be fast, offline-resilient, CA-verified, and beautiful to use.

The Fragmented Legacy Approach

The Pain Points We Solved

  • Outlets freeze when internet drops, creating cashier panics and angry customer queues.
  • Event & banquet reservations managed on spreadsheets, causing double bookings and missed Banquet Orders (BO).
  • Night audits require hours of manual data export to reconcile restaurant receipts against room folios.
  • Financial statements fail USALI standard audits, creating tax and investor scrutiny.
The Safari Unified Standard

The Safari Engineering Breakthrough

  • 100% Offline Continuity: Barcode scanning, weighing scales, and cash drawers never stop.
  • Complete Event Solution: Reservations, custom quotations, items, packages, and BO automated to kitchen and bills.
  • Real-Time Cross-Sync: Dining, retail, and event charges post immediately to guest room folios.
  • CA-Certified USALI Ledger: Departmental P&L schedules (Rooms, F&B, Admin) update automatically with zero manual entry.
